Philly Cheesesteak

Photo by Tochukwu Ekeh

Description

Looking for a classic American sandwich that will leave you wanting more? A Philly cheesesteak should do the trick!

Ingredients

  • 1⁄2 tsp salt
  • 1⁄2 tsp black pepper
  • 1⁄2 tsp paprika
  • 1⁄2 tsp chili powder
  • 1⁄2 tsp onion powder
  • 1⁄2 tsp dried thyme
  • 1⁄2 tsp dried marjoram
  • 1⁄2 tsp dried basil
  • 1 lb beef sirloin, cut into 2 in strips
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 green bell pepper, julienned
  • 3 oz Swiss cheese, thinly sliced
  • 4 hoagie rolls, split lengthwise

Directions

  1. Mix salt, pepper, paprika, chili powder, onion powder, garlic powder, thyme, marjoram, and basil together in a small bowl.
  2. Place steak in a large bowl; sprinkle seasoning mixture over top and stir to coat.
  3. Heat 1⁄2 of the oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add steak in batches if needed to avoid overcrowding the skillet; cook until browned and just cooked through. Transfer cooked steak to a plate.
  4. Heat the remaining oil in the skillet. Add onion and green pepper; cook and stir until tender and caramelized.
  5. Preheat the oven on the broiler setting. Divide cooked beef between the bottoms of 4 rolls.
  6. Layer with onion and green pepper. Top with sliced cheese. Place on a cookie sheet.
  7. Broil in the preheated oven until cheese is melted.
  8. Cover with tops of rolls and serve.
